# Audience Comments

Collect representative comments from the audience. Focus on comments that reveal emotional reactions, not just generic praise.

## High-Value Comments

Copy 10-20 comments that show:
- **Emotional reactions**: "太爽了", "终于扬眉吐气", "看哭了"
- **Recognition**: "这就是我", "你懂我", "太真实了"
- **Behavioral signals**: "我反复看了5遍", "必须转发", "收藏了"
- **Questions/Confusion**: "这是真的吗?", "后来呢?"
