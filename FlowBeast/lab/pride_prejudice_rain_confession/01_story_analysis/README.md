# 01_story_analysis/

Deconstruct the original novel chapter (Chapter 34 of *Pride and Prejudice*).

## Output Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `source_text.md` | Original Austen chapter text (public domain) |
| `cultural_context.md` | Regency England social dynamics: class, marriage, reputation |
| `story_analysis.md` | Structural breakdown: beats, character arcs, themes, conflict |

## Core Questions

1. Why does Darcy confess?
2. Why does Elizabeth refuse?
3. What is the real conflict of this scene?
4. Why is the audience moved?
