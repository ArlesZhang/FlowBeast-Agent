## How to Use These Prompts

1. **Copy the prompt** for each shot
2. **Paste into AI generation tool** (Jimeng/Seedance/Kling)
3. **Add negative prompts** (from negative_prompts.md)
4. **Set parameters** (16:9, 24 fps, 1080p+)
5. **Generate and review** for consistency
6. **Regenerate if needed**
