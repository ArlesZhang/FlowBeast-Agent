# Script: Darcy's Rain Confession
**Based on:** Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 34  
**Duration:** ~2 minutes  
**Format:** AI Manju (漫剧)

---

## Scene 1: The Unexpected Arrival
**Location:** Hunsford Parsonage, sitting room  
**Time:** Late afternoon, overcast  
**Characters:** Elizabeth (alone), Darcy (enters)  
**Weather:** Gentle rain begins

---

[ACTION: Elizabeth sits by window, reading. Rain patters softly against glass. She looks up as door opens suddenly.]

[ACTION: Darcy stands in doorway, coat dripping. He's breathing hard, as if he's been running. His eyes find Elizabeth immediately.]

**ELIZABETH** [emotion: surprised, slightly alarmed]
"Mr. Darcy. I didn't expect—"

[ACTION: She rises, book forgotten. He steps inside, closes door. The room feels smaller.]

**DARCY** [emotion: intense, barely contained]
"Miss Bennet. I must speak with you."

[ACTION: He removes his gloves slowly, deliberately. His hands tremble slightly. He doesn't look away from her.]

---

## Scene 2: The Confession
**Location:** Same  
**Weather:** Rain intensifies

---

[ACTION: Darcy paces to window, then turns to face her. He takes a breath, as if steeling himself.]

**DARCY** [emotion: desperate, vulnerable]
"In vain have I struggled."

[ACTION: Elizabeth's eyes widen. She takes a step back.]

**DARCY** [emotion: pain, longing]
"It will not do. My feelings will not be repressed."

[ACTION: He steps closer. She doesn't move. The distance between them shrinks.]

**DARCY** [emotion: raw, honest]
"You must allow me to tell you how ardently I admire and love you."

[ACTION: Silence. Rain drums harder on the windows. Elizabeth's hand goes to her throat.]

**ELIZABETH** [emotion: shocked, disbelieving]
"You... love me?"

**DARCY** [emotion: desperate, pleading]
"Against my will. Against my reason. Against my family's wishes."

[ACTION: He gestures helplessly, as if his own words surprise him.]

**DARCY** [emotion: pain, frustration]
"But I cannot fight it any longer."

---

## Scene 3: The Obstacles
**Location:** Same  
**Weather:** Heavy rain, occasional thunder

---

[ACTION: Darcy paces again, running a hand through his wet hair. He's fighting for control.]

**DARCY** [emotion: defensive, proud]
"Your family's situation... your mother's behavior... your sisters' impropriety..."

[ACTION: Elizabeth's expression hardens. Her hands clench at her sides.]

**DARCY** [emotion: struggling, conflicted]
"I have fought against these obstacles. Against my better judgment."

[ACTION: He turns to her, eyes burning.]

**DARCY** [emotion: intense, almost pleading]
"And yet I love you. Despite everything."

[ACTION: Elizabeth's eyes flash with anger. She takes a step forward.]

**ELIZABETH** [emotion: furious, wounded]
"Despite everything? You call my family improper while declaring your love?"

[ACTION: Darcy flinches, as if struck.]

**ELIZABETH** [emotion: building rage]
"You think you're making a sacrifice? You think I should be grateful?"

---

## Scene 4: The Accusations
**Location:** Same  
**Weather:** Storm outside, thunder crashes

---

[ACTION: Elizabeth stands tall, pointing accusingly. Darcy recoils.]

**ELIZABETH** [emotion: righteous fury]
"You separated my sister from the man she loves."

**DARCY** [emotion: defensive, shocked]
"I did what I thought was—"

**ELIZABETH** [emotion: cutting him off, fierce]
"You broke her heart!"

[ACTION: She steps closer, voice rising.]

**ELIZABETH** [emotion: moral certainty, anger]
"You destroyed Mr. Wickham's prospects. Ruined an innocent man's life."

**DARCY** [emotion: stunned, pained]
"Wickham is not what he—"

**ELIZABETH** [emotion: explosive, final]
"And you have the arrogance to stand here, in this modest parsonage, and tell me you love me despite my family's impropriety?"

[ACTION: Darcy's face goes pale. He takes a step back.]

**ELIZABETH** [emotion: devastating, final]
"You are the last man in the world whom I could ever be prevailed on to marry."

[ACTION: Silence. Thunder crashes outside. Darcy stands frozen, as if the words have physically struck him.]

---

## Scene 5: The Departure
**Location:** Same  
**Weather:** Rain continues, softer now

---

[ACTION: Long pause. Darcy's face is a mask of controlled devastation. He straightens his coat with trembling hands.]

**DARCY** [emotion: humiliated, dignified]
"And this is your opinion of me."

[ACTION: Elizabeth doesn't answer. She's breathing hard, adrenaline still pumping.]

**DARCY** [emotion: formal, pained]
"I thank you for explaining it so fully."

[ACTION: He bows stiffly, formally. It's the bow of a man who has lost everything but his dignity.]

**DARCY** [emotion: quiet, devastated]
"I wish you happiness."

[ACTION: He turns and walks to the door. His steps are measured, controlled. He doesn't look back.]

[ACTION: The door closes softly. Elizabeth stands alone in the center of the room, trembling.]

[ACTION: She sinks into a chair, hands covering her face. The rain continues to fall.]

[ACTION: Fade to black. Rain sound continues.]

---

## End

**Total Duration:** ~2 minutes  
**Total Dialogue:** ~280 words  
**Emotional Arc:** Tension → Vulnerability → Pride → Fury → Devastation → Silence

---

## Production Notes

### Key Visual Moments
1. **Darcy's trembling hands** when he says "In vain have I struggled"
2. **Elizabeth's eyes widening** as she realizes what he's saying
3. **His pacing** during the confession (internal struggle made visible)
4. **Her pointing accusation** during "last man in the world"
5. **His rigid bow** before leaving (dignity barely intact)
6. **Her collapse** into the chair after he's gone (adrenaline crash)

### Rain as Emotional Metaphor
- **Beat 1:** Gentle rain (tension building)
- **Beat 2:** Heavy rain (confession, vulnerability)
- **Beat 3:** Storm (accusations, anger)
- **Beat 4:** Thunder crash (refusal, climax)
- **Beat 5:** Softer rain (aftermath, exhaustion)

### Eye Contact Patterns
- **Start:** Brief, awkward (he enters)
- **Middle:** Intense, confrontational (confession, accusations)
- **End:** Avoidance (he doesn't look back, she covers her face)

### Physical Proximity
- **Start:** Distance (doorway to center of room)
- **Middle:** Close (he steps toward her, she doesn't move)
- **Climax:** Very close (accusations, confrontation)
- **End:** Distance again (he leaves, she's alone)

### Subtext (What They Don't Say)
- **Darcy doesn't say:** "Please say yes. I can't bear this vulnerability."
- **Elizabeth doesn't say:** "Why does your rejection hurt more than it should?"
- **Neither says:** "There's an attraction here we're both fighting."

### Emotional Beats for Voice Direction

**Darcy:**
- "In vain have I struggled" → desperate, vulnerable, raw
- "Against my will, against my reason" → pain, frustration, longing
- "Your family's impropriety" → defensive, proud, struggling
- "I wish you happiness" → formal, pained, devastated

**Elizabeth:**
- "You love me?" → shocked, disbelieving, confused
- "Despite everything?" → furious, wounded, defensive
- "You broke her heart!" → righteous fury, protective
- "Last man in the world" → explosive, final, devastating

---

## Character Consistency Notes

### Darcy Throughout
- **Posture:** Rigid, controlled (even when vulnerable)
- **Gestures:** Minimal, deliberate (trembling hands break through)
- **Voice:** Formal, measured (emotion breaks through in key moments)
- **Eyes:** Intense, direct (except when leaving)

### Elizabeth Throughout
- **Posture:** Starts seated, rises, stands tall (moral authority)
- **Gestures:** Animated, expressive (pointing, clenching hands)
- **Voice:** Clear, direct (rises in anger, wavers slightly at end)
- **Eyes:** Wide (shock), narrow (anger), downcast (aftermath)

---

## Success Metrics

This script succeeds if:
1. **The iconic lines land** (audience remembers "In vain" and "last man")
2. **The emotional arc is clear** (tension → vulnerability → fury → devastation)
3. **Both characters are sympathetic** (Darcy's vulnerability, Elizabeth's principles)
4. **The subtext is visible** (attraction beneath conflict)
5. **The rain works** (emotional metaphor, not just weather)
6. **The pacing works** (slow burn to explosive climax to exhausted silence)
7. **Total duration is ~2 minutes** (300 words dialogue + action)
