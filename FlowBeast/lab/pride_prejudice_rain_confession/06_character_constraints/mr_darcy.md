# Character Bible: Mr. Darcy (Fitzwilliam Darcy)
**Source:** Pride and Prejudice by Jane Austen  
**Scene:** Darcy's Rain Confession (Chapter 34)  
**Age:** 28 years old

---

## Physical Appearance

### Face
- **Shape:** Strong jawline, aristocratic but not severe
- **Features:** Handsome, striking, memorable
- **Eyes:** Dark, intense, penetrating
- **Eyebrows:** Dark, expressive (often furrowed)
- **Nose:** Straight, strong, aristocratic
- **Mouth:** Firm lips, often set in serious expression
- **Complexion:** Fair, clear (wealthy, well-cared-for)
- **Hair:** Dark brown/black, thick, slightly wavy

### Build
- **Height:** 6'0" (tall for the period)
- **Build:** Athletic, broad-shouldered, strong
- **Posture:** Rigid, upright, controlled
- **Movement:** Measured, deliberate, controlled

### Distinguishing Features
- **Eyes:** Intense, penetrating, often brooding
- **Jawline:** Strong, set when tense
- **Hands:** Large, strong (tremble when vulnerable)
- **Voice:** Deep, measured, formal

## Expression Range

### Default Expression (This Scene)
- **Start:** Intense, barely contained emotion
- **Middle:** Vulnerable, desperate, proud
- **End:** Humiliated, dignified, devastated

### Key Emotional Expressions

**Internal Struggle (S04, S06):**
- Jaw clenched
- Eyes intense
- Breathing slightly rapid
- Hands clench/unclench

**Vulnerability (S10, S12, S15):**
- Eyes soft, pleading
- Voice breaks slightly
- Hands tremble
- Posture less rigid

**Pride (S19, S22, S24):**
- Chin raised
- Eyes intense, almost challenging
- Voice firm, defensive
- Gestures controlled

**Shock (S27, S30, S33):**
- Eyes widen
- Step back
- Face pales
- Momentary stillness

**Humiliation (S39, S41, S45):**
- Eyes downcast (briefly)
- Face pale, controlled
- Jaw set tight
- Posture rigid (dignity barely intact)

**Devastation (S44, S45, S46):**
- Eyes empty, pained
- Movements slow, measured
- Voice quiet, formal
- Bow stiff, formal

- **Controlled:** Minimal, deliberate

## Voice and Speech

### Vocal Qualities
- **Pitch:** Deep, resonant
- **Tone:** Formal, measured, controlled
- **Pace:** Deliberate, careful
- **Volume:** Rises with emotion, softens with devastation

### Speech Patterns (This Scene)
- **Start:** Formal, strained ("Miss Bennet")
- **Middle:** Desperate, raw ("In vain have I struggled")
- **Defensive:** Proud, condescending ("Your family's impropriety")
- **End:** Formal, pained ("I wish you happiness")

## Emotional Arc (This Scene)

### Beat 1: Intense Struggle
- Barely contained emotion
- Desperation to confess
- Nervous energy

### Beat 2: Desperate Vulnerability
- Confession breaks through
- Raw, honest emotion
- Pleading, hoping

### Beat 3: Defensive Pride
- Lists obstacles (his "sacrifice")
- Believes he's being magnanimous
- Unaware of his condescension

### Beat 4: Shocked Humiliation
- Stunned by refusal
- Absorbs accusations
- Dignity barely intact

### Beat 5: Controlled Devastation
- Formal departure
- Dignity maintained
- Internally broken

## Psychological Profile

### Core Traits
- **Proud:** Wealthy, powerful, used to deference
- **Principled:** Strong moral code (though sometimes misguided)
- **Reserved:** Doesn't express emotion easily
- **Loyal:** Devoted to family, friends
- **Intelligent:** Perceptive (though blind in this moment)

### Flaws (This Scene)
- **Proud:** Frames love as sacrifice, condescension
- **Blind:** Doesn't see how his words insult her
- **Arrogant:** Expects gratitude, not rejection
- **Defensive:** Can't hear her accusations without defending

### Strengths (This Scene)
- **Vulnerable:** Risks rejection by confessing
- **Honest:** Speaks his feelings directly
- **Dignified:** Maintains composure even when devastated
- **Respectful:** Accepts her refusal, doesn't argue

## Visual Consistency Rules

### Always Include
- **Dark brown/black hair** (thick, slightly wavy, damp)
- **Dark, intense eyes** (penetrating, expressive)
- **Strong jawline** (set when tense)
- **Dark tailcoat** (wet from rain)
- **Upright posture** (rigid, controlled)
- **Large, strong hands** (tremble when vulnerable)

### Never Include
- **Blonde or light hair** (must be dark)
- **Light eyes** (must be dark)
- **Casual clothing** (must be formal, upper-class)
- **Slouching** (must be upright, even when emotional)
- **Modern elements** (no modern hairstyles, clothing, accessories)

### Common AI Mistakes to Prevent
- **Face changes:** Ensure face remains consistent across shots
- **Hair color drift:** Keep dark, not gray or light brown
- **Coat changes:** Same wet coat throughout scene
- **Expression mismatch:** Ensure expression matches emotional beat
- **Posture inconsistency:** Upright when standing, not slouching
- **Dry coat:** Coat must stay wet throughout (he doesn't remove it)
