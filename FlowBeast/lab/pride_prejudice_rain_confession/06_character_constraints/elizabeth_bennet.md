# Character Bible: Elizabeth Bennet
**Source:** Pride and Prejudice by Jane Austen  
**Scene:** Darcy's Rain Confession (Chapter 34)  
**Age:** 20 years old

---

## Physical Appearance

### Face
- **Shape:** Oval, delicate but not fragile
- **Features:** Fine, expressive, intelligent
- **Eyes:** Dark brown, bright, sparkling with wit
- **Eyebrows:** Arched, expressive
- **Nose:** Straight, refined
- **Mouth:** Full lips, quick to smile (but not in this scene)
- **Complexion:** Fair, clear, natural (no heavy makeup)
- **Hair:** Dark brown, thick, wavy

### Build
- **Height:** 5'4" (average for the period)
- **Build:** Slender but not delicate, healthy, active
- **Posture:** Upright, confident, animated
- **Movement:** Quick, graceful, expressive gestures

### Distinguishing Features
- **Eyes:** Her most striking feature — bright, intelligent, expressive
- **Smile:** Quick, witty (but suppressed in this scene)
- **Hands:** Slender, expressive (gestures when speaking)
- **Voice:** Clear, direct, capable of sharp wit

## Costume (This Scene)

### Dress
- **Style:** Regency empire-waist gown (early 1810s)
- **Color:** Soft blue-gray (modest, practical)
- **Fabric:** Cotton or light wool (middle-class, not silk)
- **Neckline:** Square or modest V-neck
- **Sleeves:** Short puffed sleeves (typical of period)
- **Skirt:** Floor-length, slightly A-line
- **Details:** Minimal decoration (small ribbon at waist, no elaborate trim)

### Accessories
- **Shawl:** Light wool, draped over shoulders (cool afternoon)
- **Jewelry:** None or minimal (small earrings, no necklace)
- **Hair:** Simple style, partially down, small pins
- **Shoes:** Flat slippers (not visible in most shots)
- **Gloves:** None (she's at home, informal)

### When Wet
- **Not applicable:** Elizabeth stays dry (inside parsonage)
- **If rain blows in:** Dress darkens slightly, hair may have a few damp strands
- **Shawl:** May pull it closer if draft from door

## Expression Range

### Default Expression (This Scene)
- **Start:** Calm, curious, slightly alarmed
- **Middle:** Shocked, angry, defensive
- **End:** Exhausted, trembling, doubtful

### Key Emotional Expressions

**Surprise (S03, S05):**
- Eyes widen
- Mouth slightly open
- Eyebrows raised
- Head tilted slightly

**Shock (S11, S13):**
- Eyes very wide
- Mouth open
- Hand to throat
- Step back

**Anger (S20, S25, S28):**
- Eyes narrow
- Lips pressed together
- Jaw clenched
- Pointing finger

**Fury (S29, S32, S36):**
- Eyes blazing
- Mouth open (shouting)
- Gestures sharp, accusatory
- Stepping forward

**Exhaustion (S48):**
- Eyes downcast
- Shoulders slumped
- Hands covering face
- Sinking into chair

## Body Language

### Posture
- **Start:** Seated, relaxed, reading
- **Middle:** Standing tall, confrontational
- **End:** Seated again, collapsed

### Gestures
- **Calm:** Hands in lap, minimal movement
- **Shocked:** Hand to throat, step back
- **Angry:** Hands clench, pointing
- **Furious:** Pointing, stepping forward, sharp gestures
- **Exhausted:** Hands cover face, shoulders slump

### Movement Patterns
- **Reading:** Still, focused
- **Surprised:** Rises quickly
- **Shocked:** Steps back
- **Angry:** Steps forward
- **Confrontational:** Very close, pointing
- **Aftermath:** Sinks into chair

## Voice and Speech

### Vocal Qualities
- **Pitch:** Medium, clear
- **Tone:** Direct, articulate, capable of sharp wit
- **Pace:** Quick when emotional, measured when controlled
- **Volume:** Rises with anger, softens with exhaustion

### Speech Patterns (This Scene)
- **Start:** Polite, formal ("Mr. Darcy")
- **Middle:** Direct, accusatory ("You separated my sister")
- **Climax:** Explosive, sharp ("Last man in the world")
- **End:** Silent (no dialogue)

## Psychological Profile

### Core Traits
- **Intelligent:** Quick-witted, perceptive (usually)
- **Independent:** Values her own judgment
- **Principled:** Won't accept condescension
- **Loyal:** Defends her family fiercely
- **Proud:** Values her perceptiveness

### Flaws (This Scene)
- **Prejudiced:** Judges Darcy harshly based on first impressions
- **Blind:** Believes Wickham's lies without question
- **Certain:** Too sure of her own judgment
- **Defensive:** Can't hear Darcy's love without hearing insult

### Strengths (This Scene)
- **Principled:** Refuses to be a "sacrifice"
- **Courageous:** Stands up to wealthy, powerful man
- **Loyal:** Defends Jane and Wickham
- **Honest:** Speaks her mind directly

## Visual Consistency Rules

### Always Include
- **Dark brown hair** (wavy, partially down)
- **Dark brown eyes** (bright, expressive)
- **Empire-waist dress** (blue-gray, modest)
- **Upright posture** (confident, even when emotional)
- **Expressive hands** (gestures when speaking)

### Never Include
- **Blonde or light hair** (must be dark brown)
- **Blue or green eyes** (must be dark brown)
- **Elaborate gowns** (must be modest, middle-class)
- **Heavy makeup** (must be natural)
- **Jewelry** (must be minimal or none)
- **Modern elements** (no modern hairstyles, makeup, accessories)

### Common AI Mistakes to Prevent
- **Face changes:** Ensure face remains consistent across shots
- **Hair color drift:** Keep dark brown, not black or light brown
- **Dress changes:** Same dress throughout scene
- **Expression mismatch:** Ensure expression matches emotional beat
- **Posture inconsistency:** Upright when standing, not slouching (until Beat 5)
