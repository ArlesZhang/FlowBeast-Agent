# Style Lock: Darcy's Rain Confession
**Target Platform:** Jimeng AI (即梦) / Seedance / Kling  
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9  
**Visual Style:** Semi-realistic anime with cinematic quality

---

## Visual Style Definition

### Style Category
**Semi-realistic anime with cinematic lighting**

This is NOT:
- Pure anime (too stylized)
- Photorealistic (too flat)
- Painterly (too abstract)
- 3D render (too artificial)

This IS:
- Detailed, expressive character art
- Cinematic lighting and composition
- Emotional facial expressions
- Period-accurate costumes
- Atmospheric rain and weather effects

### Style Influences
- **Makoto Shinkai** (Your Name, Weathering with You) — atmospheric rain, emotional lighting
- **2005 Pride & Prejudice film** (Joe Wright) — period accuracy, romantic tension
- **1995 BBC Pride & Prejudice** — character authenticity
- **Studio Ghibli** — detailed backgrounds, expressive characters
- **Cinematic anime** (Violet Evergarden, A Silent Voice) — emotional storytelling

### Mood
- **Romantic tension** (attraction beneath conflict)
- **Melancholic beauty** (rain, gray light, emotional storm)
- **Period elegance** (Regency costumes, modest parsonage)
- **Emotional intensity** (confession, refusal, devastation)

## Lighting Approach

### Key Light
- **Source:** Overcast sky through window
- **Quality:** Soft, diffused, no harsh shadows
- **Direction:** From window (left or right side)
- **Intensity:** Moderate (not bright, not dark)
- **Color:** Cool gray-blue (storm-light)

### Fill Light
- **Source:** Reflected light from walls, furniture
- **Quality:** Very soft, subtle
- **Direction:** Opposite key light
- **Intensity:** Low (fills shadows slightly)
- **Color:** Warm brown (reflected from wood)

### Backlight/Rim Light
- **Source:** Window (when character is between camera and window)
- **Quality:** Soft edge light
- **Direction:** Behind character
- **Intensity:** Moderate (creates silhouette effect)
- **Color:** Cool gray (storm-light)

### Special Lighting
- **Thunder flashes (S28, S38):** Brief white/gray flash, then return to normal
- **Rain highlights:** Water droplets catch light subtly
- **Fireplace:** Unlit, no warm light
- **Candles:** Not lit, no artificial light

## Camera Rules

### Allowed Movements
- **Static shots** (79% of shots) — stability, focus on performance
- **Slow push-in** (13% of shots) — increasing emotional intensity
- **Tracking shots** (4% of shots) — following character movement
- **Pan shots** (4% of shots) — revealing space

### Forbidden Movements
- **Handheld shake** — too unstable for period drama
- **Fast zooms** — too modern, too aggressive
- **Dutch angles** — too stylized, breaks immersion
- **360° spins** — too dynamic, not appropriate
- **Crane shots** — too complex for this intimate scene

### Lens Preferences
- **35mm:** Wide shots (establishing, both characters)
- **50mm:** Medium shots (natural perspective, dialogue)
- **85mm:** Close-ups (portraits, emotional moments)
- **100mm:** Extreme close-ups (details: hands, eyes)

### Frame Rates
- **24 fps:** All shots (cinematic feel)
- **No slow motion:** Real-time performance
- **No fast motion:** Natural pacing

## Composition Rules

### Rule of Thirds
- Place characters at intersection points
- Don't center characters (unless intentional for emphasis)
- Use negative space for mood (isolation, tension)

### Leading Lines
- Window frame (vertical lines)
- Door frame (vertical lines)
- Furniture edges (horizontal lines)
- Rain streaks (vertical lines on window)

### Depth
- **Foreground:** Rain on window (blurred)
- **Midground:** Characters (sharp)
- **Background:** Room details (soft focus)

### Balance
- Two characters: Balance frame with both
- Single character: Use negative space for mood
- Objects: Furniture, props create visual weight

## Atmospheric Effects

### Rain
- **Visible on windows:** Streaks, droplets, blurred background
- **Audible:** Constant sound, varying intensity
- **Lighting effect:** Soft, diffused, cool
- **Emotional effect:** Isolation, passion, cleansing

### Thunder
- **Timing:** Matches emotional peaks (S28, S38)
- **Lighting:** Brief flash, then return to normal
- **Sound:** Distant rumble → closer → crash → fading
- **Emotional effect:** Amplifies conflict

### Shadows
- **Soft:** Diffused light creates soft shadows
- **Direction:** From window (key light)
- **Depth:** Subtle, not dramatic
- **Mood:** Melancholic, intimate

### Mist/Fog
- **Not applicable:** Indoor scene, no mist
- **If visible through window:** Very subtle, distant
