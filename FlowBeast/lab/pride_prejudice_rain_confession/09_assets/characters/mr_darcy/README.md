## CRITICAL: Wetness Continuity

Darcy's coat and hair MUST stay wet throughout the entire scene. When creating reference assets:
- Show the coat as clearly wet (darkened fabric, water droplets)
- Show the hair as damp (slightly tousled, clinging)
- Show boots as wet and muddy
- This is NOT optional — it's the most commonly broken continuity rule

## Visual Reference (From Character Bible)

### Face
- Strong jawline
- Dark, intense eyes
- Dark brown/black hair (thick, slightly wavy, damp)
- Fair complexion (clear, well-cared-for)
- Handsome, striking features

### Costume
- Dark blue/black tailcoat (WET from rain)
- Cream waistcoat
- White high-collared shirt
- White or cream cravat
- Light-colored trousers (beige or gray)
- Black Hessian boots (wet, muddy)
- Leather gloves (removed, held in hand)
